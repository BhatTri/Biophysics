VesEvoL Vesicle Evolution over Long time

Tanmay Pandey, Rajni Kudawla, Jeyanth Varshan B S, Chetana Devarakonda, Aritra Chatterjee, Tripta Bhatia

Description: An image-analysis framework for time-lapse microscopy (CZI / TIFF stacks) that follows vesicles over extended time periods. It detects the boundary of a large "mother" vesicle in every frame, detects and tracks the smaller "daughter" vesicles (StarDist, Cellpose, trackpy), and classifies mother–daughter and daughter–daughter contacts. It also provides curvature, normalised-area, polar and segmentation-validation analyses, plus figure generation.

Publication:
  1. Evolution of Vesicle Shape Parameters Based on A Deep-Learning Based Image Analysis Framework Over Extended Time Periods. Pandey et al., bioRxiv (2026)

Membrane Mechanics

Avneesh Singh, Ridima Singh, Tripta Bhatia

Description: An interactive analysis for Giant Unilamellar Vesicles (GUVs). Compute angular auto-correlations and extract the bending rigidity κ and effective surface tension Σ via Helfrich / Brochard–Lennon theory.

Spherical Harmonics Explorer

Avneesh Singh, Ridima Singh, Tripta Bhatia

Description: Interactive software that visualises spherical harmonic functions Ylm(θ, φ). Users can explore how the quantum numbers l and m affect the shape, symmetry and nodal structure of the functions on a sphere.
